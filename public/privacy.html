1. Who We Are

2-Minute Sermon Ministry ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website at www.2minutesermon.org. We are a Christian ministry dedicated to delivering short, scripture-rooted devotional messages from ordained ministers worldwide.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our site or interact with our ministry online.

2. What Information We Collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide to us. We do not run advertising trackers, sell data, or build user profiles.

  • Email Address — When you subscribe to our weekly devotional newsletter or register for updates.
  • Name & Email (Prayer Requests) — When you submit a prayer request. Your name is optional; email is requested for pastoral follow-up only.
  • Name, Email & Message (Contact Form) — When you use our Contact page to reach the ministry team.
  • Administrator Content — If you are an administrator using The Steward (admin portal), we store content you create (sermons, verses, events) in our secure cloud database.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Newsletter email — To deliver weekly devotional messages you signed up for. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.
  • Prayer request email & name — Shared confidentially with our ministerial prayer team only. We will never publish your prayer request publicly without your explicit consent.
  • Contact form submissions — Reviewed by our ministry team to respond to your inquiry. We retain these for a reasonable period to support our response.
🙏 We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for commercial purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website relies on a small number of trusted third-party services to operate. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google) — We use Firebase Hosting to serve this website and Firebase Firestore as our content database. Google's Privacy Policy →
  • Formspree — Our contact and prayer forms are processed by Formspree, which forwards submissions to our ministry email inbox. Formspree Privacy Policy →
  • YouTube (Google) — Sermon videos on our site are embedded from YouTube. YouTube may set cookies or collect data when you interact with video embeds. Google's Privacy Policy →
  • Google Fonts — Our site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address as part of the font delivery request.

5. Data Storage & Browser Storage

Our site uses your browser's localStorage (built-in browser storage) to remember content preferences — for example, seasonal display settings or the last daily verse you viewed. This data stays entirely on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Content administrators also use localStorage as a local cache for the CMS. All published content is securely synchronized to our Firebase cloud database.

6. Cookies & Tracking

We do not use advertising cookies, analytics tracking cookies, or any third-party tracking pixels on our website.

Your browser may receive cookies related to YouTube video embeds (from Google) when you play a sermon video. These are controlled by YouTube/Google, not by us. You can manage or clear these cookies through your browser settings.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your personal data from our records.
  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email we send.
  • Ask us to stop using your prayer request for any further follow-up communications.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the form at 2minutesermon.org/#contact.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is a general ministry resource not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please reach out:
