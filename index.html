Governance & Framework

2-Minute Sermon Structure

Our platform operates through three foundational pillars ensuring faithful governance, pastoral vetting, and creative broadcast excellence.

Governance
🏛️

The Board

The platform is governed by its Board, entrusted with the pivotal task of making strategic decisions. The Board comprises the President, Executive Coordinator, Administrative Coordinator, Finance & Fundraising Coordinator, along with Members who serve as Consultants and Advisors.

Pulpit & Fellowship
🎙️

Preachers Network (PN)

The Preachers Network within 2-MS serves as a framework for recruiting individuals who possess a divine calling to preach and teach the Word of God, facilitating their regular participation on the platform. The primary aim of the PN is to foster connection, consultation, and the sharing of faith among preachers affiliated with 2-MS. Network Preachers contribute to each theme and collectively discern semester themes through prayerful consultation.

Creative & Broadcast
🎥

Media Branch

The Media branch serves as the creative arm of our online preaching platform, responsible for crafting visually engaging content to promote our messages. This dynamic team designs posters, develops video templates, and ensures our digital broadcast presence effectively captures the essence of our mission and vision.

Leadership & Ministers

Meet the Team

The platform is led by an active Board of Executives followed by the Network Preachers.
