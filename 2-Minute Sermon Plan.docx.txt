Platform Recommendation and Why Primary choice:  Webflow  +  Donorbox Design parity:  pixel-level control to reproduce NextGen hero, grids, and CTAs. CMS:  structured collections for sermons, series, events, and team. Maintenance:  hosted platform with SSL and automatic updates. Donations:   Donorbox  embeds provide recurring gifts, receipts, and easy integration. Editor experience:  visual editor with role-based access and safe editing. Alternate choice: WordPress (managed host) +  GiveWP Choose only if you require advanced donor CRM, complex receipts, or many plugin integrations. Higher maintenance and security overhead. QA, SEO, Accessibility and Analytics Checklist Accessibility:  alt text for images; color contrast >= 4.5:1 for body text; keyboard navigation; transcripts for videos. SEO:  meta titles and descriptions; Open Graph images; sitemap; structured data for sermons. Analytics:  install Google Analytics 4; set donation conversion events; add Facebook Pixel if used. Performance:  lazy load images; compress media; test Lighthouse scores. Security:  enable 2FA for editors; strong passwords;  Webflow  SSL. Risks and Mitigation Risk:  Donation receipts or CRM needs exceed  Donorbox  capabilities.  Mitigation:  Integrate  Donorbox  with a donor CRM via Zapier or choose WordPress +  GiveWP . Risk:  Editors accidentally break layout.  Mitigation:  Use  Webflow  Editor roles and provide a short style guide and training. Risk:  SEO traffic loss.
