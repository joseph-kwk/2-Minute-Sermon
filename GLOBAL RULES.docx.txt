GLOBAL RULES Keep  medium radius  everywhere (buttons, cards, dropdowns, footer tiles). Use  Sermon  Red (# C62828)  as the primary accent. Use  Deep  Charcoal (# 1A1A1A)  for strong backgrounds. Use  White (#FFFFFF)  and  Off White (#FAFAFA)  for clean content areas. Use  Light  Gray (# EAEAEA)  for subtle separators. Use  Inter  for all text;  Playfair Display  only for H1/H2. 🔻  EXPLORE DROPDOWN (Daily Verse, Preachers, Events, FAQ) Your Explore dropdown should feel  light, modern, and clean , not heavy. Dropdown Background White (#FFFFFF)  Why: keeps it airy, readable, and consistent with your header. Dropdown Border 1px Light  Gray (# EAEAEA)  Why: subtle separation without heaviness. Dropdown Shadow Soft shadow  (Wix default) Why: gives depth without clutter. Dropdown Text Deep Charcoal (#1A1A1A)  Why: high readability, matches logo typography. Hover State Background:  Sermon  Red (# C62828) Text:  White (# FFFFFF)  Why: strong brand identity, clear interaction. Item Spacing 12–16px vertical padding  Why: clean separation, easy tapping on mobile. Item Radius Medium radius  (same as your site) Why: consistency across UI. FOOTER (coloring, styles, background) Your footer should feel  solid, trustworthy, and brand anchored . Footer Background Deep  Charcoal (# 1A1A1A)  Why: anchors the page, contrasts with your red logo, feels premium. Footer Text White (# FFFFFF)  Why: maximum readability on charcoal. Footer Link Hover Sermon  Red (# C62828)  Why: ties footer interaction to brand identity. Footer Dividers 1px Light  Gray (# EAEAEA)  Why: subtle structure without clutter. Footer Icons White  or  Sermon Red  depending on importance Why: keeps visual hierarchy clean. Footer Button (Newsletter CTA) Background:  Sermon  Red (# C62828) Text:  White (#FFFFFF) Radius:  Medium Hover:  # A32020  (slightly darker red) Why: strong CTA that matches your logo. ✉️  NEWSLETTER SECTION (coloring, background, style) This section should feel  inviting , not heavy.
